An AI-made music video in the style of a swung 1970s Saturday-morning educational cartoon, about AI features that arrive switched on. Claude Code (Opus 5.5) wrote every frame as code, as SVG cutout rigs rendered in Remotion; Suno V6 made the music; Claude drafted the words. Switch is voiced by a real child, recorded at home and comped into the song by the creator, who also mixed and mastered it. Otherwise a human only picked takes, approved gates and supplied the real material; every such action is logged in [log/human_interventions.md](https://github.com/Derivative-Work/default-on/blob/main/log/human_interventions.md).

AI-generated satire about real events. Every real product on screen is cited in a caption, and the fact cards after the song give the sources. Every on-screen claim was audited by ChatGPT and Gemini and each flag checked against its source: [log/audits/03_review.md](https://github.com/Derivative-Work/default-on/blob/main/log/audits/03_review.md). Conflict of interest: Claude, made by Anthropic, drafted the lyrics, and the first fact card names Anthropic's own default-on toggle; see the [README](https://github.com/Derivative-Work/default-on#default-on).

**Watch:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRdWKtGsklg

The main video here is a re-encode of the CRF 16 render (2.7 GB, over GitHub's 2 GiB file limit) at YouTube's recommended 4K bitrate. Every other render and source file is listed with SHA-256 hashes in [MANIFEST.sha256](https://github.com/Derivative-Work/default-on/blob/main/MANIFEST.sha256), and the [production log](https://github.com/Derivative-Work/default-on/blob/main/log/production_log.md) has the command for each.

Not included, to protect the child who voices Switch: his raw takes, his isolated lines, the stems and the effects-only mix, and the Claude Code session transcript (it holds private family details).
