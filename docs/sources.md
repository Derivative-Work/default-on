## F1: Answer box on top; tiny "Web" tab

Google's AI Overviews rolled out broadly from I/O 2024. Google's help page calls them a core Search feature that can't be turned off. The "Web" filter shows plain results.

**Status:** Fact

- Google Search Help: https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/14901683
- Google (May 14, 2024): https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/generative-ai-google-search-may-2024/

## F2: Inbox helper flipped on last fall

Thele v. Google alleges Gemini-powered smart features were switched on by default for Gmail, Chat and Meet from about October 10, 2025. Google says it changed no settings and doesn't train Gemini on Gmail content.

**Status:** Alleged. Dismissed for lack of standing July 7, 2026, with leave to amend; refiled August 18; Google's new motion to dismiss pending

- ModemGuides: https://www.modemguides.com/blogs/ai-news/turn-off-gmail-ai-smart-features
- PPC Land: https://ppc.land/google-faces-refiled-gemini-gmail-suit-after-judge-found-no-concrete-harm/
- Court docket, N.D. Cal. 5:25-cv-09704: https://dockets.justia.com/docket/california/candce/5:2025cv09704/459505

## F4: TV tile you can hide but not delete

A December 2025 LG webOS update added a Copilot shortcut that could only be hidden. LG said it would let owners delete it in a future update. Microsoft says Copilot leaves LG and Samsung TVs on December 1, 2026.

**Status:** Fact; LG's promised fix unconfirmed

**Status:** Alleged; case before the Federal Court

## F9: Correction; "deeply integrated"

Google then said schools can turn Gemini off and keep Search, but AI Overviews and AI Mode can't be removed from Search. Changes begin in early 2027. On Nov. 3, 2026, Google's VP for education, Chris Phillips, had told The New York Times that Search and AI are "deeply integrated," so students at schools that reject its AI "will not have access to search." Context: in August 2026 Google had switched Gemini on by default for many K-12 student accounts; in October, Senators Blumenthal and Hawley asked Google why it makes AI the default in schools.

**Status:** Fact
