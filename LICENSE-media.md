## What this covers

- The video in every cut: the main video, the two vertical cuts, the animatic, the drafts and every intermediate
  render.
- The music as mixed: the final master of "Default On" (the Suno V6 track, mixed and mastered by the creator) and the
  credits music (a second Suno V6 track).
- The lyrics, captions (`captions/*.srt`), the closing cards and the thumbnail.
- The SVG illustrations drawn in code (`remotion/src`), as pictures: Switch, the Narrator, the kids, the grey hand, the
  sets, the props and every other drawing. The code that draws them is MIT; the frames it renders are CC BY-NC-SA.
- Stills and contact sheets under `review/` and `archive/`.
- The production record, written by the agent: the brief's mirror, logs, prompts, timing map, upload metadata, and
  the other documents under `docs/`, `log/`, `timing/` and `lyrics/`.

## What this doesn't cover

- **Code** (`remotion/src/`, `scripts/` and the build files): MIT, see [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- **Switch's voice.** Switch is voiced by a child. His lines appear in the video, inside the final mix, under this
  licence as part of the video, with his guardians' consent (`log/human_interventions.md`). His recordings are not
  separately licensed: the raw takes, his isolated vocal, the clicks and the room tone are kept privately and are not
  in this repository, its releases or its manifest.
- **The real footage** (S67, `assets/real/S67/`): the creator's own footage of a small hand on a light switch. It
  appears in the video under this licence as part of the video; the file is kept locally, hashed in
  `MANIFEST.sha256`, and not separately licensed.
- **The screen recordings** (S65–S66, `assets/real/S65/`): the creator's own recordings of their Suno workspace and
  their Logic Pro project. The interfaces on them are Suno's and Apple's. The files are kept locally, hashed in
  `MANIFEST.sha256`, and not in this repository.
- **Sound effects** in the mix: "people crowd shouting" and "film special effects record scratch" from Pixabay
  (Pixabay Content License) and a horn from Logic Pro's sound library (Apple's licence). Their licences allow them
  inside a production, not on their own, so they are covered only as part of the video, and the FX-only file is never
  published. The door and switch sounds are the creator's own recordings.
- **Fonts:** Titan One, Work Sans and Kalam, each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 by their authors. They are
  installed from npm (Fontsource) and are not in this repository.
- **The planning chat** (`docs/planning_chat/`): a snapshot of the human's public share of a Claude conversation,
  archived verbatim.
- **Suno's page data** (`log/prompts/suno/`): the public share page and its responses, saved as the record of the take.
- **The reference clips** the human supplied for the look (1970s educational shorts): studied as sampled stills for
  line, colour and grade only, never copied into the video, and never in this repository.
